Wordbook explains vocabulary like a patient teacher instead of displaying a dense dictionary entry. Most words use concise, reviewed explanations bundled with the app, including a natural example and an optional memory aid tailored to the word's meaning and spelling, so everyday study works quickly and offline. For an uncommon spelling that is not in the bundled collection, Wordbook may ask its service for a reviewed explanation and cache the result on this device. The on-device language model is reserved for interactive learning features such as checking a learner's own explanation; Wikipedia and web links remain optional ways to explore a word, not sources for the main lesson.
