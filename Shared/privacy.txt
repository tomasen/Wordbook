Wordbook Privacy Policy

Effective August 24, 2026

Wordbook is designed so that its core study experience can work from data stored on your device. This policy explains what stays on your device, what may leave it, and why.

Information stored on your device

Wordbook stores your saved words, study history, answers, familiarity levels, settings, cached reviewed explanations, and pending explanation feedback on your device. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to normal device backups and any iCloud data you chose to keep.

iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, Apple stores and synchronizes your Wordbook study data across your Apple devices. That data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy terms and is not exposed to Wordbook as an identifiable user account.

Reviewed explanation service

Wordbook looks in its bundled reviewed explanation library and local cache first. When an exact spelling is not available locally, or when you confirm that an unusual spelling is correct, the app may send the following to Wordbook's service:

- the spelling you entered;
- language and locale;
- app, content, schema, and resolver versions; and
- an opaque request identifier used for that operation.

The service uses this information to return a reviewed explanation, a spelling suggestion, a temporary pending status, or an honest negative result. Rare lookup forms and completed reviewed explanations may be retained so that the same request can be answered faster and included in a future offline content pack.

Explanation feedback

If you mark an explanation helpful or request a better explanation, Wordbook may send opaque Entry, Usage, explanation, and feedback-event identifiers; the rating and affected part; rejected explanation identifiers; whether a replacement was requested; and app/content contract versions. Feedback is used to deliver a requested replacement and to improve future reviewed content. If delivery is temporarily unavailable, the app keeps the event in a local outbox and retries it later with the same event identifier.

Wikipedia and external discovery

When you open a word on iPhone or iPad, Wordbook may request a short summary from Wikimedia using that spelling. A failure does not prevent the reviewed explanation from working. News, image, web, and translation links contact the selected external provider only when you choose them. Those providers apply their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Purchases and restorations are processed by Apple. Wordbook receives the purchase status needed to unlock features but does not receive your full payment-card details.

Network operations and tracking

Hosting infrastructure necessarily processes an Internet Protocol address and basic request metadata long enough to deliver and secure a network request. Wordbook does not sell personal information, show behaviorally targeted advertising, combine this data with third-party data, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Study data remains on your device or in your private iCloud account until you remove it. Unlinked service records are retained only as needed to provide, secure, review, and improve the explanation library. Because service request and feedback identifiers are not tied to a Wordbook account, it may not be possible to associate an individual unlinked record with you. For privacy questions or a deletion request concerning information that can be identified, contact tomasen@gmail.com.

Children

Wordbook is not directed to children under 13, and Wordbook does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact tomasen@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Wordbook's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with the current version.
